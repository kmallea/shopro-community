# Shared Roles and Links

ShoPro creates synchronized views for collaborators who need different levels of access during a production.

| Role | Best for | Capabilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Producer | Director or show caller | Advance segments, manage live structure, view notes and scripts, create highlights |
| Host | Primary presenter | Advance segments, view notes and scripts, create highlights |
| Guest Host | Panelist or contributor | View notes and scripts, create highlights |
| Audience | Client, observer, or public viewer | Follow a simplified rundown without private notes or controls |

The server determines the role from the link's access token. Changing a role name in the browser does not grant additional permissions.

## Sharing safely

- Give each person the lowest-privilege role that meets their needs.
- Treat role links like credentials.
- Do not paste role links into public issues, screenshots, or recordings.
- Regenerate a role token when a link is exposed or access should end.
- Agree on one person who will advance segments to avoid double-advances.

## Connection summary

Shared views display aggregate connection counts by role. The summary helps the team confirm that expected participants are present without displaying their identities.

## Password-protected and legacy links

A password-protected share requires the configured password. Older public share links receive Audience access only. For current productions, prefer explicit role links from the Share panel.
