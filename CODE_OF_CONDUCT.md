# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making the ShoPro community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free place for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role in live production.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the substance of a disagreement.
- Give actionable feedback and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect privacy, credits, boundaries, and production confidentiality.
- Focus discussions on improving ShoPro and helping its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Publishing another person's private information
- Sharing private ShoPro links, credentials, production notes, or recordings without permission
- Spam, deliberate disruption, or sustained off-topic behavior

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these guidelines. Enforcement decisions will consider context, severity, and the safety of the community.

To report conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through their GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive personal details in a public issue.
